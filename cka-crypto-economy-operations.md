## 2. AI-Agent Operations Model

### Core Principle

AI agents run repeatable, data-driven operations. Humans handle trust-building, legal judgment, creative strategy, and anything with irreversible financial consequences above a threshold.

### Role Allocation Matrix

| Function | AI-Handled | Human-Handled | Notes |
|----------|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Tier-1 customer support | Yes | Escalation only | AI resolves 80%+ of tickets |
| Content moderation | Yes | Appeals | AI flags, auto-removes clear violations, queues edge cases |
| Review quality scoring | Yes | Audit samples | Fully automated scoring; human spot-checks weekly |
| Social media posting | Yes | Strategy & tone | AI drafts and schedules; human approves campaigns |
| Analytics & reporting | Yes | Interpretation | AI generates; human decides what to act on |
| Marketplace operations | Yes | Vendor relationships | AI manages listings, categories; human handles key accounts |
| Dispute resolution | Triage + recommendation | Final decision | AI gathers evidence and proposes resolution; human approves payouts |
| Fraud detection | Detection + blocking | Investigation | AI flags and can auto-block below threshold; human investigates |
| Treasury monitoring | Monitoring + alerts | Decisions | AI never moves funds; human authorizes all transactions |
| Governance facilitation | Summarize + present | Vote outcomes | AI summarizes proposals; community votes; human implements |

### Agent Specifications

#### Moderation Agent

- **Purpose:** Maintain marketplace content quality and safety
- **Inputs:** New listings, reviews, user-generated content, reported items
- **Actions:** Auto-approve clean content, flag edge cases, auto-remove clear violations, score review authenticity
- **Model:** Claude (Haiku for speed on bulk moderation, Sonnet for nuanced cases)
- **Autonomy:** Level 1 for clear violations (spam, slurs, obvious scams); Level 2 for borderline cases
- **Metrics:** False positive rate, average review time, appeal overturn rate
- **Estimated volume:** 500-5,000 items/day depending on growth phase

#### Support Agent

- **Purpose:** Handle customer and vendor inquiries
- **Inputs:** Support tickets (Telegram, in-platform chat, email)
- **Actions:** Answer questions, look up order status, guide onboarding, triage disputes, escalate
- **Model:** Claude Sonnet (conversational quality matters)
- **Autonomy:** Level 1 for information requests; Level 2 for anything involving refunds or account changes
- **Metrics:** Resolution rate, average response time, escalation rate, CSAT
- **Estimated volume:** 50-500 tickets/day depending on phase

#### Analytics Agent

- **Purpose:** Monitor platform health and generate insights
- **Inputs:** Transaction data, user metrics, token metrics, market data
- **Actions:** Generate daily/weekly reports, detect anomalies, forecast trends, alert on KPI deviations
- **Model:** Claude Sonnet + custom data pipelines
- **Autonomy:** Level 1 (reporting only, no actions)
- **Outputs:** Dashboard updates, Slack/Telegram alerts, weekly PDF reports
- **Key metrics tracked:**
  - Marketplace: GMV, active users, vendor count, average order value
  - Tokens ($KDEX, $KRUNE, $KSHD): price, volume, velocity, holder distribution, Gini coefficient
  - Health: fraud rate, dispute rate, churn, NPS

#### Marketing Agent

- **Purpose:** Community engagement and content creation
- **Inputs:** Content calendar, platform events, community feedback, trending topics
- **Actions:** Draft social posts, schedule content, respond to community, generate blog drafts, track engagement
- **Model:** Claude Sonnet for drafting; scheduling via Buffer/Hootsuite API
- **Autonomy:** Level 1 for pre-approved templates; Level 2 for new campaigns or reactive content
- **Platforms:** Twitter/X, Discord, Telegram, blog, newsletter

#### Treasury Agent

- **Purpose:** Monitor protocol treasury and token reserves
- **Inputs:** On-chain data, DEX liquidity, treasury wallet balances, fee accrual
- **Actions:** Generate reports, alert on anomalies (large withdrawals, liquidity drops, unusual patterns), never move funds
- **Model:** Claude Haiku + custom on-chain monitoring scripts
- **Autonomy:** Level 1 only (monitoring and alerting, zero transaction authority)
- **Alerts:** Telegram notifications to founder for any anomaly
- **Reports:** Daily balance summary, weekly treasury report, monthly health assessment

#### Governance Agent

- **Purpose:** Facilitate DAO governance processes
- **Inputs:** Proposals submitted by KarmaRune ($KRUNE) holders, voting results, discussion threads
- **Actions:** Summarize proposals in plain language, present pros/cons analysis, publish voting results, track implementation of passed proposals
- **Model:** Claude Sonnet (nuance in summarization matters)
- **Autonomy:** Level 1 for summarization and presentation; Level 3 for implementation (human executes)
- **Active:** Years 3-5 when DAO is live

### Human Oversight Checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Frequency | Who | What |
|-----------|-----------|-----|------|
| Moderation audit | Weekly | Founder / Community Manager | Review 20 random AI moderation decisions, check false positive/negative rates |
| Support quality review | Weekly | Founder | Read 10 random support conversations, check tone and accuracy |
| Financial reconciliation | Daily | Founder | Verify treasury balances match reports, check for unauthorized movements |
| Analytics validation | Monthly | Founder + Advisor | Confirm metrics methodology is sound, check for data quality issues |
| AI performance review | Monthly | CTO / Founder | Model accuracy, cost efficiency, latency, error rates |
| Escalation review | Weekly | Founder | Review all Level 2 and Level 3 escalations, check response time |

### Escalation Paths

```
User issue
    │
    ▼
AI Support Agent (responds in < 2 min)
    │
    ├── Resolved → Log + close
    │
    ├── Needs human → Telegram alert to Founder / Community Manager
    │                   Response SLA: 4 hours (Year 1), 1 hour (Year 2+)
    │
    └── Urgent (fraud, safety, legal) → Immediate Telegram alert + auto-freeze
                                         Response SLA: 30 minutes
```

### Brand Identity

**Platform name:** **TradeKarma** (tradekarma.net)

**Token ecosystem:**

| Token | Ticker | Role | Description |
|-------|--------|------|-------------|
| **KarmaDex** | $KDEX | Payment / Transaction | The primary medium of exchange on the marketplace |
| **KarmaRune** | $KRUNE | Reward / Reputation | Earned through positive behavior: honest reviews, quality products, community participation |
| **KarmaShard** | $KSHD | Store of Value / Vault | Long-term holdings, staking, treasury reserves |

**Brand tone:**
- Trustworthy, not hype-driven
- Community-first, not investor-first
- Transparent about everything: treasury, tokenomics, team, decisions
- Anti-rug-pull: vesting schedules, multi-sig wallets, public audits, no anonymous team
- Educational, not jargon-heavy
- Professional enough for merchants, approachable enough for consumers

**Key messaging:**

> "Reward good, don't punish bad."

- Current e-commerce platforms punish bad actors (bans, delistings, review removal). This creates an adversarial relationship between platform and users.
- TradeKarma rewards good behavior: honest reviews, quality products, responsive service, community participation.
- KarmaRune ($KRUNE) aligns incentives: when you act well, you earn. When the community thrives, your tokens gain value.
- TradeKarma: Swiss-built, community-governed, fully transparent.

**Visual identity direction (brief for designer):**
- Color palette: warm greens, golds, and whites (growth, value, clarity)
- Avoid: neon crypto aesthetics, dark mode defaults, aggressive gradients
- Logo: simple, clean, should work as a favicon and a token icon
- Typography: modern sans-serif, readable, not techy
- Overall feel: "A marketplace you'd trust your grandmother to use, powered by technology she doesn't need to understand."
