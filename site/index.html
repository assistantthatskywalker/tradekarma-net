The Problem

E-Commerce Is Broken.

Today's marketplaces are built on punishment — catching bad actors after the damage is done. The people who create real trust get nothing for it.

01

Fake Reviews Everywhere

Fake reviews undermine purchase decisions. We want to test rewards for verified, useful contributions, independently of whether a review is positive.

02

Vendors Are Disposable

Great service earns no lasting advantage. Reputation is locked to one platform, and an algorithm change can wipe out a vendor's visibility overnight.

03

Communities Own Nothing

Buyers and vendors build the trust that makes a marketplace valuable — then watch every bit of that value get extracted by the platform that hosts it.

Earning Mechanics

Not All Actions Are Equal.

KarmaRune is logarithmic, quality-weighted, and capped. The system pays for the exact behavior that helps a real person make a real decision.

01

Logarithmic, not linear

The base reward decreases with a participant's review count. Purchase value does not increase review rewards. This curve still needs anti-farming controls.

diminishing returns
02

Detail multiplies your reward

In the reference formula, a new user's first detailed photo review on an unreviewed product earns 117 KRUNE; a basic review in that same situation earns 30.

quality multiplier
03

Helpfulness pays retroactively

A verified helpful answer earns 5 KRUNE. The commerce integration must establish usefulness; self-declared votes do not establish evidence.

delayed bonus
04

First reviews earn 3×

The first reviews on a product — where coverage is thin — earn a 3× bonus. Go where the trust doesn't exist yet and get rewarded for building it.

coverage bonus

How It Works

Four Steps to Earned Trust.

From your first purchase to real yield — a loop that rewards the people who create trust, not just the platform that hosts it.

STEP 01

Browse & Buy

Shop quality vendors. Every honest action from here on is the start of a reputation you actually own.

STEP 02

Earn $KRUNE

Honest reviews, on-time shipping, helpful answers, referrals. Reputation you build — never buy.

STEP 03

Stake KRUNE + KDEX

The reference contract holds KDEX for 90 days and references KRUNE in your wallet. Top-ups restart the entire lock; there is no early exit.

STEP 04

Redeem or Recycle

Only transferred fees can fund rewards. The reference USDC redemption path exists; KDEX conversion is disabled by default. No return is guaranteed.
