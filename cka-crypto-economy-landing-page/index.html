The Problem

E-Commerce Is Broken

Platforms punish bad behavior but never reward the good. Reviews are gamed, vendors get deplatformed overnight, and communities own nothing.

Fake Reviews Everywhere

30% of online reviews are fabricated. Platforms can't tell the difference because reputation has no cost and no weight. Trust is a marketing budget, not a track record.

Vendors Are Disposable

Years of work wiped out by an algorithm update. No portable reputation, no recourse. Amazon, Etsy, and Shopify own your customers, your reviews, your identity.

Communities Own Nothing

You write reviews, answer questions, build the platform's value — and get nothing. No equity, no governance, no share in the upside. Platforms extract; communities give.

Earning mechanics

Not All Actions Are Equal

KRUNE earning is logarithmic, quality-weighted, and capped. Bigger purchases earn more, but not proportionally. Better reviews earn disproportionately more.

Purchase scaling

Logarithmic, not linear

A $100 purchase earns 15 base KRUNE. A $1,000 purchase earns 30 — not 150. Hard cap prevents whales from grinding reputation through spending.

Review quality

Detail multiplies your reward

The same purchase earns 4x more KRUNE with a detailed photo review than a one-liner. AI scores specifics, comparisons, and real usage context.
